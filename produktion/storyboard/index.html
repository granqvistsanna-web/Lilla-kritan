Lilla Kvack: Leka boll

Kvack balances her red ball, calls Nalle on a toy phone and meets him at the playground. A bumblebee knocks their ball into a tree. Nalle's stomp can't shake it loose, the bee cheers Kvack up, and Nalle's throw knocks it down. They play until the bus takes him home. Then she falls asleep under the moon. Sound effects

Each scene lists its cues in window.SFX. assembly/cues.mjs reads them into sfx-cues.json, and mix-sfx.py lays them on the episode timeline, cutting the used part of each take and rotating between variants.

How it is built

Per scene

  1. Animate in code-shot/<scene>/index.html (SVG + JS, seeked frame by frame).
  2. Render to silent.mp4, 1920×1080: node ~/.claude/skills/launch-video/scripts/render.mjs code-shot/<scene>
  3. Check stills and stills/sheet.jpg in the same folder.

Whole episode

  1. assembly/build-draft.sh joins the scenes with iris.sh (crayon iris wipe) and straight cuts → videos/episode-draft3-silent.mp4.
  2. Reads every scene's cues, mixes the effects, adds jingle, piano and outro, limits to −0.4 dBFS → videos/episode-draft3.mp4.
