## Beskrivning (publicerad)

```
Welcome to Tiny Crayon TV! Here, crayon drawings turn into songs and stories for the littlest ones, about 1–4 years old.

A crayon draws, and the world comes to life. The little red bus picks up its friends, Lil Quack plays with a ball, and a frog, a duck, a mouse and lots of other friends join in.

In every episode, one thing happens at a time. There is lots of repetition, and pauses where your child can sing along, count, toot, wave and say the words. Nothing is scary, and when something gets tricky, friends help out.

All the songs are our own, with simple words that are easy to learn.

New episodes with songs about colors, vehicles, animals and letters are on the way. Subscribe so you don't miss one!
```
