    A single round red apple for a toddler picture book, premium soft wax crayon and oil pastel, neat and controlled, not messy. Bright warm red with an even, smooth colour across the front and one small soft lighter red highlight on the upper left, a small brown stem and one green leaf on top. A slightly chubby, friendly, almost round shape with a gently flattened bottom so it can stand. NO face, no eyes, no mouth, no cheeks: leave the middle of the apple plain so a face can be added later. NO dark outline: instead a thick, clean, even, smooth white sticker border all around the whole silhouette, like a die-cut vinyl sticker. Facing the viewer, centered, full apple with space around, on a pure solid white background, no shadow, no ground, no text.
