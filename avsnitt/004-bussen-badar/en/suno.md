# Suno – 004 Let's Go Swimming! (English)

Två sånger, rad för rad som de svenska tagningarna (`../rader.txt` och `../rader-badplatsen.txt`), så att scen A och scen B kan tajmas om efter de nya tagningarna. Båda är fristående prompter (ingen Extend behövs).

## 1. Scen A

Title: Let's Go Swimming!

Style: slow calm intro with only soft keys and voice, the groove starts after the intro, gentle funky toddler song, 100 bpm, groovy bouncy funk feel, soft round bass line, clean funky rhythm guitar, light tight drum groove with soft kick and rimshot, warm clavinet, soft brass stabs on "toot-toot", friendly childlike female vocal, playful, warm, clear English diction, quiet instrumental breaks, sing every section in order, nothing skipped, three full choruses

Exclude: distortion, rock, heavy drums, loud drops, sudden loud parts, shouting, autotune, rap, tempo changes, sound effects, swedish

Lyrics:
[Intro - slow and soft, only keys and voice]
The crayon draws, round and yellow …
what can it be?
What can it be?

A sun! A sun that shines so warm,

## 2. Scen B, badplatsen

Title: And Now We Swim!

Style: gentle funky toddler song, 100 bpm, groovy bouncy funk feel, friendly childlike female vocal, warm and playful, clear English diction, relaxed groovy funk jam, soft round bass line, clean muted funky rhythm guitar, warm clavinet, light tight drums with soft kick and rimshot, soft brass stabs on "toot-toot", glockenspiel sparkles on the splashes, short sung lines with small instrumental gaps between them, call and response, playful, sunny, even dynamics, soft slow ending, about 60 seconds

Exclude: distortion, rock, heavy drums, loud drops, sudden loud parts, shouting, autotune, rap, tempo changes, sound effects, long intro, long solo, swedish

Lyrics:
[Intro - groove, 2 bars]
And now we swim!
And now we swim!

[Verse - tiptoe, stop-time, small gap after each line]
First the toes – plip, plip, plip!
[Break - 1 bar]
Then the feet – plip, plip, plip!
[Break - 1 bar]
One, two, three – hop, hop, hop!

[Jam - 4 bars, groovy, funky guitar and clavinet, happy]
Splash, splash!
Splash, splash!

[Chorus]
And now we swim, splash, splash, splash,
and now we swim, you and me!

[Break - 1 bar, soft]
The bus wants to swim too,
toot-toot, toot-toot!

now the bus is swimming too!

[Outro - slower, waving, gap after each line]

[Break - 1 bar]
