# Suno – 007 The Brown Song (English)

Omtagning 2026-11-06: första tagningen hoppade över kotten, bryggan och hela refräng 2. Delarna är nu numrerade och Style ber om alla delar i ordning.

From version 8, calypso (take 5, the one used in the rough cut), line for line, so the scenes can be re-timed to the new take.

Title: The Brown Song

Style: sunny toddler calypso pop, 92 bpm, bouncy and happy, steel drums, ukulele, marimba, soft bass, shaker and bongos, glockenspiel sparkles. Vocals: a warm female lead singer, soft and smiling, clear English diction, sings simply and slowly for 2-year-olds, "brown" sung long like browwn. A bright childrens choir echoes every line in parentheses and sings along on every chorus. Catchy chorus: "slide down in caramel" is the hook, sung twice on the same bouncy melody, a slide whistle glides down on wheee, splashes on splash, kids count one two three louder and louder. Bicycle horn on toot toot, steel drum shimmer before each chorus. Journey: soft ukulele-only intro, the verse gently builds, a short dreamy hush before each chorus, then every chorus bouncy and full with the same melody, verse 2 drops to quiet ukulele and bass and sounds a little worried, verse 3 bursts back happy, the final chorus is the biggest sing-along, short soft ending. Sing every section in order, nothing skipped: two full choruses before the final chorus.

Exclude: rock, heavy drums, loud drops, screaming, autotune, rap, reggaeton, country, banjo, tempo changes, fast tempo, long solo, swedish
