# Suno – 003 The Bus Goes Visiting (English)

Aim for 2:45–3:00. The [Instrumental Break]s are where the characters' lines go; the [Break]s in the intro and verse 4 are ~3 s questions to the child.

Style:
Gentle sweet toddler song, steady 96 BPM, bouncy. Marimba plucks, ukulele, soft pads, tiny shakers, toy horn on "toot-toot". Friendly warm female vocal, playful. Clear English diction, real instrumental gaps where marked, short gentle ending.
Exclude: heavy drums, electric guitar, autotune, rap, tempo changes, long intro, long outro, swedish

Lyrics:
[Intro]
Crayon draws, round, round, round,
what can it be?
[Break]
A wheel! A wheel that spins around,
now off we go!

[Instrumental Break]

[Verse 1]
Here comes the bus, so round and red,
toot-toot, she says hello!
Who is sitting, swinging legs?
The bunny smiles and waves – hello!

[Chorus]
Toot-toot, toot-toot,
little bus goes for a spin!
Toot-toot, toot-toot,
spin, spin, spin – come hop in!

[Instrumental Break]

[Verse 2]
One, two, three – hop-hop!
Into the bus she hops.
In the window: hop-hop, hop-hop!
Off we go – no stops!

[Chorus]
Toot-toot, toot-toot,
little bus goes for a spin!
Toot-toot, toot-toot,
spin, spin, spin – come hop in!

[Instrumental Break]

[Verse 3]
Vroom-vroom, down the road,
past an apple tree.
The tractor's ploughing, chug-chug-chug,
and the bus toots happily!

[Instrumental Break]

[Verse 4 - soft]
The lamb is sleeping by the hay,
snore … peep … snore … peep.
What does the lamb say? Can you say?
[Break]
Baa-baa! A little eye peeks out!
Let's sing some more: baa-baa, baa-baa,
good morning, little lamb!

[Verse 5]
Everyone's on the bus now,
we drive around the farm.
Toot-toot and hop-hop, baa,
and we wave: hi, hi, hi!

[Instrumental Break]

[Verse 6]
Vroom-vroom, off again,
past a little pond.
The plane flies high, high, high,
and the tractor waves: hello!

[Instrumental Break]

[Verse 7]
Last stop, a little kiosk,
and the frog says: ice cream!
One for the bunny, one for the lamb,
and the bus says: toot!

[Final Chorus]
Toot-toot, toot-toot,
we went on a long, long ride!
The lamb woke up, we had ice cream,
toot-toot and bye-bye!
[End]
