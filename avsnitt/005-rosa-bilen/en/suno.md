# Suno – 005 The Pink Song (English)

Line for line the same structure as the Swedish suno.md, so the scenes can be re-timed to the new take. The edit notes in ../suno.md (4–5 s question gaps, ~15 s kiosk gap) still apply.

Title: The Pink Song

Style: super catchy funky toddler song, 104 bpm, bouncy groovy funk, soft round slap-style bass line, clean muted funky rhythm guitar, warm clavinet, tight light drums with soft kick and rimshot, handclaps on every "Pink!", soft brass stabs on "toot, toot", call and echo with a small happy kids choir answering in the parentheses, friendly childlike female lead vocal, warm and smiling, clear English diction, short shoutable hook "Pink!" repeated many times, sing-along, small instrumental gaps, even dynamics, slow soft keys-only intro

Exclude: distortion, rock, heavy drums, loud drops, sudden loud parts, screaming, autotune, rap, tempo changes, sound effects, long solo, long intro, swedish
