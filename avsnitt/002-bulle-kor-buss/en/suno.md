# Suno – 002 The Bear Drives the Bus (English)

Style:
Gently swaying toddler song, steady 96 BPM, 4/4, feels like riding a bus. Ukulele, soft bass, marimba, light shaker, toy horn on "toot-toot", a wooden knock sound on every "knock, knock". Warm friendly female lead, small children's choir on the chorus. Playful, warm, clear English diction, short intro, short gentle ending.
Exclude: heavy drums, electric guitar, autotune, rap, tempo changes, long intro, long outro, swedish

Lyrics:
[Chorus]
Vroom, vroom, vroom, here we go,
wheels are spinning, you and me.
Toot-toot, we're rolling slow –
who are we going to see?

[Verse 1 - knock knock]
Knock, knock, knock on the door,
who lives in the little red house?
Knock, knock – the door opens –
it's the mouse, the little grey mouse!

[Chorus]
Vroom, vroom, vroom, here we go,
wheels are spinning, you and me.
Toot-toot, we're rolling slow –
who are we going to see?

[Verse 2 - knock knock, softer]
Knock, knock, knock on the door,
The chick lives here, we know it's true!
Knock, knock … nobody's home –
little chick, where are you?

[Final Chorus]
Vroom, vroom, vroom, here we are,
everybody hop out – yippee!
Cake, cake on a plate,
yum for the mouse, the chick and me!
[End]
