# Suno – 001 The Ice Cream Bus (English)

Style:
Happy swaying toddler song, steady 100 BPM, 4/4. Ukulele, marimba, glockenspiel, soft handclaps, light shaker, toy horn on every "toot-toot". Warm friendly female lead, a small children's choir joins the chorus. Bouncy, simple, easy to sing along, clear English diction, short intro and short gentle ending.
Exclude: heavy drums, electric guitar, autotune, rap, tempo changes, long intro, long outro, swedish

Lyrics:
[Chorus]
Toot-toot, toot-toot,
the ice cream bus is off – let's scoot!
Wheels go spinning, spin, spin, spin,
who's that waiting? Stop! Hop in!

[Verse 1]
By the bus stop stands a mouse so grey:
"Squeak, squeak, me too! Can I ride today?"
